# Live Q&A — how to run it tonight

This is a simple tool for the fireside chat: the audience sends in their questions
from their phones, and it shows you the best ones to ask on stage — no login for
anyone, and it picks up the strongest questions automatically.

## The two links

**Audience link** — share this with everyone in the room (QR code / put it in chat):
👉 https://wispr-event.vercel.app

**Your dashboard** — open this yourself on your phone or laptop, keep it open all evening:
👉 https://wispr-event.vercel.app/host?key=form

*(Don't share the dashboard link with attendees — it's yours only.)*

## How it works

1. **Before the event**: open your dashboard link once to make sure it loads.
2. **When the session starts**: put the audience link on the screen / share it in
   chat. People open it on their phones, type or dictate their question, add their
   name, and hit send.
3. **While questions come in**: your dashboard fills up live (refreshes itself
   every few seconds — no need to reload). Each question gets a score out of 100
   automatically, so the best ones naturally float to the top.
4. **When you're ready for Q&A**: hit the big **"Curate top 10"** button at the
   top of your dashboard. It reads everything submitted and picks the best ~10
   questions to ask, with a short note on why each one made the cut. That list
   shows up in a dark box above the full feed — that's your shortlist to read from
   on stage.
5. **Fine-tuning**: on any question card you can hit **Pin** to bookmark one you
   definitely want to ask, or **Hide** to remove something irrelevant or
   duplicate from view. Hidden ones aren't gone — click **"Show hidden"** to
   bring them back if you change your mind.

## If anything looks off

- The dashboard is safe to leave open the whole time — it just keeps refreshing.
- If "Curate top 10" doesn't return anything, the full list below it is still
  live and sorted best-first — just read straight from there.
- Refreshing the page never loses any questions.
